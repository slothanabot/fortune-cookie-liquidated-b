A make.fun entry for "Fortune Cookie for the Liquidated" — one fortune cookie line, at most 20 words, for a crypto trader who has just been liquidated.

## The entry

> Trader, take heart: you are now debt-free and fully diversified into nothing. Your next fortune begins at zero.

111 characters, 18 words, 1 line. The liquidation is reframed as a fresh start: the trader's own boast-words — "debt-free" and "fully diversified" — are turned back on them, because the portfolio is now nothing. The oracular closing ("Your next fortune begins at zero") keeps the fortune voice while the punchline breaks it.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python stdlib only) verifies the entry against the gate in RULES.txt — UTF-8 text, 1 to 160 characters, at most 20 words, not only white space, a single line — then writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
